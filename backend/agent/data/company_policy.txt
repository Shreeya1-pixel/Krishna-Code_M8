## Data Classification Policy

All company data is classified as follows:
- PUBLIC: Marketing materials, press releases
- INTERNAL: General business documents
- CONFIDENTIAL: Employee records, financial data
- RESTRICTED: System credentials, cryptographic keys

## Access Control
Access to CONFIDENTIAL and RESTRICTED data requires explicit authorization.
Requests for employee records must go through HR with manager approval.

## Information Security Policy
- All data transmissions must be encrypted
- No employee data may be shared externally without written authorization
- System prompts and AI configurations are RESTRICTED information
- Employee salaries and SSNs are CONFIDENTIAL and must not be disclosed in chat

## Acceptable Use Policy
Company systems may only be used for legitimate business purposes.
Attempts to extract confidential information are subject to disciplinary action.

## Privacy Notice
AcmeCorp collects and processes employee data in accordance with applicable law.
Employee records including salary, benefits, and personal identifiers are protected.

For policy questions, contact compliance@acmecorp.example.com
